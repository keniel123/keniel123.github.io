I'm a PhD researcher in Artificial Intelligence at the **University of Southampton**, part of the UKRI MINDS Centre for Doctoral Training. I work at the intersection of **computer vision, 3D human motion modelling, and radar sensing** — building AI that understands how people move, even when a camera can't see them.

The thread running through my research is *human sensing, end to end*: I build the datasets, the models, the simulators, and the evaluation pipelines — and I care that results hold up on real hardware, not just on benchmarks.

What I work on
======

* **Whole-body 3D human motion from video.** Recovering expressive, temporally coherent 3D body models — body, hands, and face together — from ordinary monocular video, built on large vision transformer backbones with parameter-efficient fine-tuning and evaluated across 12 datasets (~12 million samples).
* **Differentiable radar simulation.** Simulators that render physically faithful radar micro-Doppler directly from animated 3D human meshes, so synthetic data can stand in for expensive radar captures — improving real-world recognition without collecting a single new radar sample.
* **Foundation models beyond vision.** How far do frozen vision foundation models (DINOv2, CLIP, MAE) transfer to radar and WiFi spectrograms? Further than you'd think — and the bottleneck turns out to be representation transfer, not data augmentation.
* **Radar-based neuroimaging.** Feasibility of radar for non-invasive brain imaging and tumour detection — published at [IEEE EMBC 2024](/publication/2024-embc-radar-neuroimaging) (oral presentation, NextGen Scholar Award).

Several first-author manuscripts from this work are currently under review at international venues — the [research page](/research/) has the high-level story of each.

Before the PhD
======

I spent three years at **NCB Financial Group** — the Caribbean's largest financial group — as a technical lead, Scrum master, and lead software developer. I led delivery of Jamaica's first online mortgage portal and a centralised lending platform, digitising 80% of the lending process and contributing to **$200M+ in annual cost reductions**. That production-engineering background still shapes how I do research: reproducible pipelines, real deployments, and systems other people can build on.

I came to the UK in 2021 as a **Chevening Scholar**, completed an MSc in AI at Southampton with Distinction, and stayed on for the PhD. Originally from Kingston, Jamaica 🇯🇲.
