I'm a PhD researcher in Artificial Intelligence at the **University of Southampton**, part of the UKRI MINDS Centre for Doctoral Training. I work at the intersection of **computer vision, 3D human motion modelling, and radar sensing** — building AI that understands how people move, even when a camera can't see them.

The thread running through my research is *human sensing, end to end*: I build the datasets, the models, the simulators, and the evaluation pipelines — and I care that results hold up on real hardware, not just on benchmarks.

<div class="home-card">
  <h2>What I work on</h2>
  <div class="home-grid">
    <div class="research-card">
      <h3>🧍 <a href="/research/">3D Humans from Video</a></h3>
      <p>Temporally coherent whole-body mesh recovery — body, hands, and face together — from ordinary monocular video, built on large vision transformers and evaluated across 12 datasets (~12M samples).</p>
    </div>
    <div class="research-card">
      <h3>📡 <a href="/research/">Differentiable Radar Simulation</a></h3>
      <p>Rendering physically faithful radar micro-Doppler straight from animated 3D human meshes, so synthetic data can stand in for expensive radar captures.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="research-card">
      <h3>🧠 <a href="/research/">Foundation Models Beyond Vision</a></h3>
      <p>How far do frozen DINOv2, CLIP, and MAE features transfer to radar and WiFi spectrograms? Further than you'd think — it's a representation-transfer problem.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="research-card">
      <h3>🩺 <a href="/publication/2024-embc-radar-neuroimaging">Radar Neuroimaging</a></h3>
      <p>Feasibility of radar for non-invasive brain imaging and tumour detection — published at IEEE EMBC 2024 (oral presentation, NextGen Scholar Award).</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <p>Several first-author manuscripts from this work are currently under review at international venues — the <a href="/research/">research page</a> has the high-level story of each.</p>

## Before the PhD

I spent three years at **NCB Financial Group** — the Caribbean's largest financial group — as a technical lead, Scrum master, and lead software developer. I led delivery of Jamaica's first online mortgage portal and a centralised lending platform, digitising 80% of the lending process and contributing to **$200M+ in annual cost reductions**. That production-engineering background still shapes how I do research: reproducible pipelines, real deployments, and systems other people can build on. Originally from Kingston, Jamaica 🇯🇲.
