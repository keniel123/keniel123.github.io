An application of **NEAT (NeuroEvolution of Augmenting Topologies)** to learning Tetris game policies: rather than fixing a network architecture and training its weights, NEAT evolves the topology and the weights together, growing complexity only when it pays off.

Multiple agent variants were implemented and compared in a Unity/C# environment, continuing a line of work that began with my BSc thesis on training intelligent agents with neuroevolution.

**Stack:** Unity, C#, NEAT

**Code:** [github.com/keniel123/neat-tetris-agent](https://github.com/keniel123/neat-tetris-agent)
