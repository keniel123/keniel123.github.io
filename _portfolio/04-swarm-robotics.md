A CDT group project, sponsored by **DSTL**, exploring how a heterogeneous robot swarm — cheap ground robots plus agile drones — can cooperatively explore disaster zones where infrastructure has failed.

**What we built**

* Cooperative exploration logic across Thymio ground robots and Tello drones
* A multi-agent coordination algorithm with a **virtual mesh network** for localisation and operator updates when fixed communications are down
* Deployment on real hardware with NVIDIA Jetson edge compute — delivered as a live physical demonstrator to the sponsor

**Stack:** Python, ROS, NVIDIA Jetson, Arduino, MQTT, Thymio robots, Tello drones
