A real-time, low-latency computer vision system that turns hand gestures into live DJ controls — volume, effects, mixing — **running entirely in the browser on a standard webcam**, with no additional hardware.

The system needs reliable sub-frame responsiveness for live interaction: a missed or late gesture is instantly audible. Getting there meant careful attention to tracking robustness, latency budgets, and gesture-to-control mapping design.

**Recognition**

* 🏆 **Best Demonstration Award**, [ECS Student Showcase 2026](/talks/2026-ecs-showcase-dj-air)
* Demonstrated as *AI DJ* at the [Southampton Science and Engineering Festival](/talks/sotsef-ai-dj), introducing camera-based gesture recognition to the public

**Stack:** real-time hand tracking, in-browser inference, webcam input, gesture-to-audio control mapping
